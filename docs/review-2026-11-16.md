## Tests

- No specs for `live-camera.component.ts`, `camera.ts`, `card-text-reader.ts` and `photo-scaler.ts`. The scan page's spec gives the camera a promise that never resolves and drives the page through `captured.emit`, so starting, stopping, capturing, "Auto" and pausing never run in a test. Findings 1 and 2 sit in that layer. Since 2026-11-16 18:25 there are specs for `live-camera.component.ts` (open, stop, pause, background, a taken camera, the side of a capture) and `camera.ts`; "Auto" finding a card is covered by the end-to-end test only. `card-text-reader.ts` and `photo-scaler.ts` stay without: they wrap browser parts the test DOM lacks (owner).
- `update.service.spec.ts` does not cover the rule that the scan page and a focused field are never reloaded. Fixed 2026-11-16 18:25.
- Three tests cannot fail (fixed 2026-11-16 18:25: the log is put in order in the document, the checkbox check is gone, `before` must be there):
  - `pages/pages.spec.ts:271-283`: the log's order comes from CSS, the test checks the order in the document.
  - `pages/card/card.page.spec.ts:622`: looks for a checkbox the page no longer has at all.
  - `e2e/cards.e2e.ts:268`: passes when `before` is empty.
- `e2e/cards.e2e.ts:290-292`: the fake camera shows the front for 3 seconds, nothing for 1, the back for 3. On a slow runner the tap can land on the back and the test still passes. Fixed 2026-11-16 18:25: the test waits for the front before the tap and checks that the stored front is yellow and the back is not. That check found a real fault: switching "Auto" on after a front taken by hand took the same side again as the back; the trigger now remembers what was taken.
- `testing/fakes.ts`: the stand-in `copiesOf` ignores the language, so "copies per language" is not tested on the card page. Fixed 2026-11-16 18:25.
